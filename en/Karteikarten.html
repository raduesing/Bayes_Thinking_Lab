📖 How do the flashcards work?

The flashcards draw live on the BTL Glossary's terms — each card shows the term name on the front, and the definition plus formula on the back.

  • Flip – Click the card or press Space.
  • Category – Pick one or more areas to practice only those terms.
  • 🔀 Shuffle – Puts the current selection into a new random order.

How does "New → Learning → Known" work?

Every card has one of three progress stages, stored only in this browser (no account, no server):

  • New – Starting state. Never confirmed with "✓ Know it" yet (or just fell back, see below).
  • Learning – You clicked "✓ Know it" exactly once. Knowing it once doesn't yet count as securely learned.
  • Known – You clicked "✓ Know it" twice in a row (confirmed once more from "Learning").

Important: A single "✗ Again" immediately sends a card back to "New" — whether it was previously "Learning" or "Known". Only your latest self-assessment counts, not an accumulated score.

The benefit: on the next round (after "🔀 Shuffle" or a category change), cards from "New" and "Learning" are shown first; "Known" ones less often — so you spend your time on what's still shaky.

Shortcuts: Space/Enter = flip, ← / → = previous/next card, once flipped: 1 = Again, 2 = Know it.
