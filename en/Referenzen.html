📚 How do I use the reference list?

This page collects all the books, articles, websites, and software packages referenced across the Bayes Thinking Lab tools — from textbooks (McElreath, Kruschke) to individual papers (e.g. on mediation analysis or IRT models).

  • Search – Type an author, title, or keyword into the search field. Cards are filtered live.
  • Category – Click a category button to see only references from that area (e.g. Causality only).
  • A–Z – Jump directly to the first entry with the chosen initial letter (sorted by author).

Each entry shows: the full citation, a short framing note, where in the Lab the source is used, and — where freely available — a link to the original source.
